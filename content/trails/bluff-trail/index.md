The Bluff Trail is the short path along the bluff just west of LMU, beside the One Westbluff neighborhood. It has views of the ocean, the marina, and the Ballona Wetlands. It is a different trail from the [Bluff Creek Trail](/trails/bluff-creek-trail/), which runs along the bluff to the east.

## About This Trail

**Route:** Out and back, about 0.9 miles round trip by the recorded track
**Difficulty:** Easy, with only about 30 feet of elevation change
**Dog-friendly:** Yes

## Bluff Trail or Bluff Creek Trail?

The two are easy to mix up because they are close together and both follow the bluff above the wetlands. The Bluff Trail is the western one, below One Westbluff. For the longer 4-mile loop with the dog parks and the three access points, see the [Bluff Creek Trail](/trails/bluff-creek-trail/). To pair a walk here with a day in the neighborhood, the [Playa Vista Parks Walking Tour](/trails/pv-tour/) covers the parks below the bluffs.
