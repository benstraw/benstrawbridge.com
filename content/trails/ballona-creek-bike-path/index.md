The Ballona Creek Bike Path follows Ballona Creek and connects the Pacific Ocean at Marina del Rey with Culver City. It is part of the Park to Playa Trail.

<!--more-->

{{< tour-map gpx="ballona-creek-bike-path.gpx" >}}

## About This Path

**Route:** Out and back, about 13.5 miles round trip by the recorded track, or about 6.7 miles each way
**Difficulty:** Easy and paved

## Where It Connects

The path meets a beach path that continues south to Redondo Beach. At the north end, Duquesne Avenue connects the bike path with the eastern portions of the Park to Playa Trail.

For official trail information, see the [LA County trails page for the Ballona Creek Bike Path](https://trails.lacounty.gov/Trail/91/ballona-creek-bike-path).
