Del Rey Lagoon is a 14-acre park in the beach neighborhood of Playa del Rey. It has lighted baseball fields, basketball courts, and a playground, and there is a parking lot for visitors.

## About This Loop

**Route:** Loop, about 0.9 miles by the recorded track
**Difficulty:** Easy, with almost no elevation change
**Dog-friendly:** Yes
**Address:** 6660 Esplanade Place, Playa del Rey, CA 90293
**Parking:** There is a parking lot at the park.
